# Kaki knowledge base

Facts checked on 22 Oct 2026 from the pages linked under each topic. Use only these facts for scheme names, amounts, fees, dates and rules.

## Hawkers' Productivity Grant (HPG), NEA
- Helps stallholders buy automation equipment and digital services (such as e-ordering, order and queue management, and kitchen management systems) to improve food preparation and service productivity.
- Co-funds up to 80% of the equipment's unit cost, excluding GST, on a reimbursement basis, capped at $7,000.
- Only equipment on NEA's Automation Equipment List qualifies. Equipment already funded by another grant is not supported. Replacing ordinary equipment like-for-like (for example a normal stove) is not what the grant covers.
- Eligibility: a valid tenancy agreement with NEA or an NEA-appointed operator, with at least 1 year of tenancy remaining, and compliance with NEA's terms.
- STATUS: the last application phase ended on 31 March 2026. The next phase has not been announced. Applications submitted before 1 April 2026 are still being processed.
- When open, apply at hawkersonline.nea.gov.sg. Enquiries: NEA 6690 6550 or nea_hpg@nea.gov.sg.
- Source: https://www.nea.gov.sg/our-services/hawker-management/programmes-and-grants

## Food Stall Licence, SFA: renewing, updating, cancelling
- Renewal fee: $32 for 1 year or $64 for 2 years. Stalls in primary schools, secondary schools and junior colleges pay $13 a year.
- SFA sends renewal reminders up to 2 months before expiry. Renew on GoBusiness (dashboard.gobusiness.gov.sg), logging in with Singpass (individuals) or Corppass (companies). The licence renews immediately on payment.
- With a GIRO arrangement, the licence renews automatically 2 months before expiry. SFA encourages GIRO.
- Renewal conditions: not barred from operating, no licence violations, and individuals or sole proprietors must have cleared any CPF MediSave arrears.
- If the licence expires, it cannot be renewed: the owner must stop operating immediately and make a new application. Operating without a valid licence is an offence.
- Keep contact details up to date on GoBusiness (free, about 3 to 5 minutes) so reminders arrive.
- The licence is not transferable and its name cannot be changed. A new owner needs a new licence.
- Get SFA approval before any structural works at the stall.
- Cancel on GoBusiness when the stall stops operating (free, approved immediately). End any GIRO with the bank too.
- Source: https://sfa.gov.sg/food-retail/licence-permit/update-renew-cancel-licence-permit-for-food-retail

## Food Stall Licence, SFA: new applications
- Needed for food stalls in hawker centres, markets, canteens, eating houses and food courts. Stalls in NEA-managed hawker centres also need a valid NEA tenancy agreement.
- Apply on GoBusiness with: the tenancy or lease agreement and its IRAS stamp duty certificate, and a layout plan of the stall with dimensions. Incomplete applications are rejected.
- Complete applications are usually approved within 7 working days. After approval, pay within 28 days or the application lapses.
- Taking over a stall: the previous licensee must cancel their licence first.
- Source: https://sfa.gov.sg/food-retail/licensing-permits/food-stall-licence

## NEA hawker stall tenancy rules
- Tenancy agreements last 36 months. Renewal is at NEA's discretion, and rent at renewal may be adjusted towards the hawker centre's median assessed market rate. Since November 2024, rent changes are staggered over a longer period.
- The stallholder must operate the stall personally (with or without assistants). The stallholder and any joint operator must each operate the stall at least 4 hours per business day.
- A stall nominee, usually an immediate family member, can be approved for reasons such as illness, national service, overseas travel, pregnancy, old age or disability. Approval takes about 4 to 6 weeks.
- Subletting or assigning (passing) the stall to someone else is not allowed.
- Each person may rent or jointly operate at most 2 NEA cooked food stalls.
- Source (secondary, summarising NEA's tender terms; tell the owner to confirm with NEA): https://dollarsandsense.sg/business/?p=13103

## Hawker assistants
- Must be 13 or older and hold a valid Food Safety Course certificate.
- Must be a Singapore Citizen, Permanent Resident, or Long-Term Visit Pass holder with a PLOC or LOC.
- Stallholders and joint operators also need a valid Food Safety Course certificate.
- Source (secondary; confirm with NEA): https://dollarsandsense.sg/business/?p=13103

## Incubation Stall Programme (ISP), NEA: for new hawkers
- For Singapore Citizens or PRs aged 21 or older, especially aspiring hawkers with no prior food stall experience.
- Must complete ITE's "Introduction to Managing a Hawker Business" course (or a similar business course), submit a short business plan, and pass a food tasting.
- Rent: 50% of market rent for the first 9 months, then 75% for the next 6 months (15 months in total). Stalls come with basic fittings and equipment.
- Apply at go.gov.sg/isp-application and pick from NEA's list of available incubation stalls.
- Source: https://www.nea.gov.sg/our-services/hawker-management/programmes-and-grants/isp

## MediSave for self-employed hawkers, CPF
- Self-employed people, including hawkers who run their own stall, must make MediSave contributions based on their age and Net Trade Income (NTI). NTI is declared by filing income tax with IRAS; no separate declaration to CPF is needed.
- After IRAS assesses the tax, CPF sends a notice of contributions (also on the CPF self-employed dashboard via Singpass). Payment is due 30 days from the notice date, by PayNow, or the owner can ask for an instalment plan.
- To apply for or renew licences, the owner must have fully paid the MediSave payable or have an active GIRO for MediSave instalments.
- For exact amounts, use CPF's Self-Employed MediSave Contribution Calculator.
- Source: https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/growing-your-savings/cpf-contributions/saving-as-a-self-employed-person

## Productivity Solutions Grant (PSG), Enterprise Singapore
- Co-funds up to 50% of qualifying costs for pre-approved solutions, such as point-of-sale (POS), inventory and other IT systems.
- Apply on the Business Grants Portal (businessgrants.gov.sg) with Corppass, before starting the purchase; most grants do not pay retrospectively. Getting quotes from several vendors is advised.
- Source (secondary; tell the owner to confirm current terms on the Business Grants Portal): https://littlebigreddot.com/singapore-sme-grants-70-percent-support-april-2026/

## Scams pretending to be government
- Official government links end in .gov.sg.
- Government agencies will never ask for bank log-in details, passwords or OTPs, or ask people to transfer money over a call or chat.
- If unsure, call the 24/7 ScamShield Helpline at 1799.
- Source: https://www.msf.gov.sg/media-room/article/response-to-motions-on-caregiver-support-and-ageing
