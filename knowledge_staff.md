# Temasek employee knowledge base (demo)

This is a DEMO for a Temasek interview. The policies below are ILLUSTRATIVE, written for the demo, and are NOT Temasek's actual policies. Only the Singapore public holidays are real.

## Leave policy (illustrative)
- Annual leave: Associates 18 days a year; Vice Presidents and above 21 days. The leave year runs Jan to Dec.
- Up to 5 unused days carry forward to the next year and must be used by 31 March, or they lapse.
- Half days (AM or PM) are allowed.
- For more than 2 consecutive days off, apply at least 5 working days in advance. Your manager can still approve shorter notice.
- All leave needs your manager's approval in the HR system. Weekends and public holidays are not counted as leave days.
- Sick leave: up to 14 days of outpatient sick leave a year, and up to 60 days including hospitalisation. A medical certificate is needed for every day of sick leave.
- Childcare leave: 6 days a year for parents of a Singapore Citizen child under 7.
- Unpaid leave: needs Director and HR approval.
- Source: Employee Handbook, section 4 (illustrative)

## Expense claims (illustrative)
- Submit claims within 60 days of the expense date, with an itemised receipt. Claims submitted by the 20th are paid with that month's salary.
- Client entertainment: up to S$150 per person (including staff present). Above that, Director approval is needed. Record the client names and company.
- Local transport: taxis and ride-hailing are claimable for client meetings, or when leaving the office after 9pm.
- Approvals by claim amount: up to S$500 your manager; S$500.01 to S$2,000 a Director; above S$2,000 a Managing Director plus Finance.
- Charge claims to your own cost centre unless the expense is for a specific deal; then use the deal code.
- Alcohol-only bills, personal items and traffic fines are not claimable.
- Source: Finance Policy FP-02 (illustrative)

## Business travel (illustrative)
- Submit a travel request at least 10 working days before departure. Travel insurance is arranged automatically once approved.
- Hotel cap per night (S$): Jakarta 250, Bangkok 230, Kuala Lumpur 220, Ho Chi Minh City 220, Manila 230, Hong Kong 400, Tokyo 380, Sydney 380, London 450, New York 500; any other city 300.
- Daily meal allowance (per diem): S$80 in Southeast Asia, S$120 elsewhere.
- Flights: economy for flights under 6 hours; business class for flights of 6 hours or more for Vice Presidents and above.
- Approvals follow the same amount limits as expense claims, based on the total estimated cost.
- Source: Travel Policy TP-01 (illustrative)

## Who to contact (illustrative)
- HR questions (parental leave, benefits, payroll errors): the HR team
- IT (password resets, laptop, access): IT service desk portal
- Finance (corporate card, reimbursement status): the Finance Operations team

## Not in this knowledge base (hand off to HR, Finance or IT)
- Pay, bonuses, promotions, performance ratings, disputes, medical details, anything about another employee, and investment or deal-specific questions.
